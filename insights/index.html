The privacy budget every AI product needs.

"We'll add privacy later" is the most expensive sentence in modern AI engineering. Once user prompts have left your perimeter unprotected, there is no later — only an incident.

A privacy budget says: this is the maximum information we are willing to leak per request, this is how we measure it, and this is who gets paged when it's crossed.

The essay walks through how to set one — what to count, what to ignore, and the three failure modes that show up before anyone notices.
